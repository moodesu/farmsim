Official house rules

3-player Hardcore Co-op Ruleset

Starting conditions

  • One shared farm for Don, Mdgi and Zakk.
  • $1,000 total starting cash.
  • No land, vehicles, buildings, livestock or productions.
  • Delete or sell all map-provided starter equipment, then correct the balance back to $1,000.
  • No starting loan.
  • Map target: No Ones Land 4X.

Game settings

  • Hard economy; seasonal growth on.
  • 3-day months; 5x normal timescale.
  • Crop destruction, weeds, lime, periodic ploughing and fieldstones on.
  • High fuel usage, vehicle damage and trailer fill limit on.
  • 1x allowed during busy fieldwork; up to 15x when genuinely waiting.
  • Sleeping allowed. Do not accelerate specifically to avoid weather or poor prices.

Stage 0 — Broke

  • Contracts and contract-supplied equipment are allowed.
  • Maximum two simultaneous contracts.
  • Contract machinery may only be used for its contract.
  • No normal leasing, loans, passive income, subsidies, productions or animals.
  • First land may only be purchased if at least $10,000 remains after purchase.

Stage 1 — Homestead

  • Begins when the first parcel is purchased.
  • Buying machinery is allowed; used equipment should be preferred.
  • Normal equipment leasing remains locked.
  • Maximum one external contract at a time after land purchase.

Stage 2 — Working Farm

  • Unlock at $150,000 farm value, with owned land, one tractor, one own field and one completed harvest.
  • Equipment leasing unlocked.
  • Loans unlocked, capped at 25% of farm asset value.
  • Prefer buying ordinary implements; lease primarily high-cost specialist equipment.

Stage 3 — Established Farm

  • Unlock at $350,000 farm value.
  • Livestock, specialist crops, silage and basic productions unlocked.
  • Additional land and larger machinery encouraged.

Stage 4 — Agribusiness

  • Unlock at $750,000 farm value.
  • Major productions, large livestock operations, extensive leasing and large-scale expansion unlocked.

Integrity rules

  • No government subsidy or equivalent passive-income cheats.
  • No teleport/resetting vehicles unless genuinely bugged.
  • Recover rolled, stuck or stranded equipment normally.
  • No exploitative leasing loops or abusing contract machinery.
  • Used machinery takes priority when a sensible equivalent is available.
  • Precision Farming becomes compulsory from Year 2 if installed.
